## 🤖 Introducing LISA—the LLM Infrastructure Sizing Agent 🚀

LISA makes it easier to explore and optimize LLM inference infrastructure through natural-language conversations and simulation-driven analysis.

Compare hardware and configurations, evaluate performance, cost, and energy, and identify the right infrastructure for your workload—all before deploying real hardware.
